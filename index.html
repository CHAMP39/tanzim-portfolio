01 / POSITION

Research depth.
Planning consequence.

I work on climate-exposed land and urban systems where decisions often outpace local monitoring.

My work examines coastal salinity, soil carbon, river-corridor change, agricultural production, and urban growth. I build spatial evidence from satellite archives, field sampling, administrative data, and planning surveys.

I have contributed to research connected with MIT’s Earth Signals and Systems Group, co-authored articles in Scientific Data and Smart Agricultural Technology, and delivered GIS analysis for public-sector and international development projects.
